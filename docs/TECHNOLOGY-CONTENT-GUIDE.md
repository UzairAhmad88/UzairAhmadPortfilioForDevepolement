# Technology Content Guide — Editorial Standards & Rules

## 1. Editorial Directives

### Prohibited Content Patterns:
- ❌ **No Skill Proficiency Ratings:** Never add `React 95%`, `Python 90%`, star ratings, or percentage progress bars.
- ❌ **No Self-Evaluative Labels:** Never label technologies with `Expert`, `Advanced`, `Intermediate`, or `Beginner`.
- ❌ **No Transitive Dependency Bloat:** Do not add every package found in `node_modules` or build plugins (e.g. `postcss`, `eslint-plugin-astro`).
- ❌ **No Rainbow Logo Walls:** Do not create visual chaos with conflicting brand colors or oversized floating logos.

### Required Content Standards:
- ✅ **Specific Architectural Role:** Clearly explain the technology's system responsibility (e.g. `Low-level dynamic tensor computing and neural network design` instead of `A popular AI library`).
- ✅ **Grounding Evidence:** Every technology must be backed by a verified project or active research inquiry in the portfolio.
- ✅ **Factual Status:** Classify status accurately (`core`, `recurring`, `specialized`, `research`) based on repository history.
- ✅ **Accessible Links:** Provide official documentation links and verified repository links for peer review.
