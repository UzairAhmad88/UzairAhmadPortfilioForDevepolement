# User Experience Baseline & Persona Audit

**Platform:** Uzair Ahmad — Personal Engineering & Research Platform  

---

## 1. Persona Evaluation Matrix

| Persona | Evaluation Question | Current Experience | Baseline Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical Recruiter** | Can they understand role, stack, and seniority in < 30 seconds? | Hero provides explicit title (`Quantitative AI & Product Engineer`), core focus strip (`PyTorch · Time-Series · Full-Stack`), and direct links to work. | **EXCELLENT** |
| **Engineering Manager** | Can they verify software engineering rigor and decisions? | Project case studies include detailed technical decisions, architecture topologies, challenges, and trade-offs. | **EXCELLENT** |
| **Quantitative Researcher** | Can they inspect mathematical formulations & methodology? | `/research` notes explain non-stationary time-series treatment, expanding windows, and GMM/HMM clustering. | **STRONG** |
| **Potential Client** | Can they understand collaboration models without agency friction? | `/services` and `/contact` present clear domain capabilities and direct conversion paths without deceptive pricing. | **EXCELLENT** |
| **General Visitor** | Can they understand the platform without deep ML jargon? | Plain-English executive summaries and problem statements precede technical sections. | **STRONG** |
