# Visual Truth & Claims Audit

## 1. Core Principle: Absolute Visual & Technical Truth

The platform strictly avoids artificial flourishes, gamified metrics, fake social proof, and exaggerated claims. Every visual artifact and statement directly corresponds to inspectable engineering work, real research inquiries, or authentic project repositories.

---

## 2. Specific Verification Items

| Category | Visual Treatment Prohibited ❌ | Authentic Implementation Enforced ✅ | Verification Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Project Metrics** | Progress bars, "skill level 95%", fake user count graphs | Verifiable architecture diagrams, runtime environments, and public GitHub repository links | VERIFIED |
| **Project DNA** | Gamified XP points, colorful gamification badges | Precise technical metadata: Classification, Role Responsibility, Context, Timeline, Deployment Runtime, Core Stack | VERIFIED |
| **Research & Lab** | AI buzzword badges, fake patent icons | Structured research questions, methodology descriptions, and working prototype state tags | VERIFIED |
| **Social Proof** | Fake client logos, fabricated customer testimonials | Real open source contributions, inspectable source code, and direct verified communication channels | VERIFIED |
| **System Status** | Flashy neon animations, fake server uptime tickers | True static status pills (`Active`, `Completed`, `Academic`, `Prototype`, `Research`, `Archived`) | VERIFIED |
| **Knowledge Engine** | Over-animated sci-fi "neural graphs" | Curated intellectual clusters and editorial reading pathways | VERIFIED |

---

## 3. Human Tone & Editorial Integrity
- The voice throughout the platform is direct, analytical, and personal.
- Case studies present the **Problem**, **Architecture & Methodology**, **Engineering Decisions**, **Results**, and **Limitations & Future Work** with intellectual honesty.
- No decorative 3D models, cursor particle effects, or AI-generated filler content exist on the platform.
