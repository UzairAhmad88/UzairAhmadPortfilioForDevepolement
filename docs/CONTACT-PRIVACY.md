# Contact Privacy & Data Retention Policy (Phase 22)

## 1. Privacy-Conscious Data Collection

The Contact System follows strict **data minimization** principles:
- **Collected Data:** Name, Email, Inquiry Type, Message, and optional organization/links provided voluntarily by the sender.
- **Excluded Data:** No tracking pixels, third-party analytics telemetry, device fingerprinting, browser extensions scraping, or invasive background cookies.

---

## 2. Storage & Retention Lifecycle

1. **No Website Database Storage:** Submissions are not retained in a web application database.
2. **Transactional Transmission:** The payload is formatted and securely dispatched to the engineer's encrypted inbox via TLS.
3. **Inbox Retention:** Inquiries are treated as private professional correspondence and managed according to standard email retention practices.
4. **No Third-Party Sharing:** Contact details are never sold, rented, or shared with marketing list brokers or advertising platforms.
