# About 2.0 System — Content & Voice Guide

## 1. Editorial Voice: Quiet Technical Confidence

The voice of the About page is that of an **analytical, product-minded systems engineer**. It prioritizes technical precision, architectural rationale, and verified evidence over promotional marketing.

---

## 2. Core Writing Rules

1. **First-Person Authenticity:** Use natural, direct first-person ("I build...", "My inquiries center on...").
2. **Concrete over Abstract:** Instead of saying "I build scalable AI apps", describe the actual mechanics: *"Engineering end-to-end prediction pipelines, fractional differencing memory preservation, and walk-forward backtesting frameworks."*
3. **No Unsubstantiated Metrics:** Never claim fake user numbers, invented funding, or imaginary performance improvements.
4. **Prohibited Vocabulary:**
   - ❌ "Passionate coder"
   - ❌ "10x rockstar developer"
   - ❌ "World-class industry leader"
   - ❌ "AI wizard / guru"
   - ❌ "Results-driven innovator"

---

## 3. Section Style Standards

| Section | Target Length | Tone & Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Hero Statement | 1–2 sentences | Precise positioning statement across quant, ML, and web systems. |
| Narrative Paragraphs | 3 structured paragraphs | Background orientation, mathematical modeling inquiries, full-stack craft. |
| Focus Areas | 1–2 sentences per card | Concrete systems and methods linking to canonical case studies. |
| Engineering Principles | 2–3 sentences per principle | Core philosophical tenet paired with its practical platform manifestation. |
| Collaboration Inquiries | 1–2 sentences per domain | Specific technical problem scopes and role alignment. |
