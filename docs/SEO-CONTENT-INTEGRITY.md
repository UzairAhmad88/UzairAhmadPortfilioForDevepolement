# SEO Content Integrity & Anti-Spam Guidelines

## 1. Grounded Content Rules
1. **Zero Artificial Keyword Lists:** Technologies and methods are mentioned only where they represent verified project stack or research tooling.
2. **Zero Fabricated Performance Claims:** Latencies, throughputs, and Sharpe ratios reported in case studies are grounded in real backtests and verified prototypes.
3. **No AI-Generated Filler Paragraphs:** Descriptions and case studies are concise, direct, and focused on architectural decisions, trade-offs, and lessons learned.

---

## 2. E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- **Experience:** Demonstrated through working GitHub repositories, verified Vercel deployments, live SSE streams, and step-by-step methodology logs.
- **Expertise:** Communicated via technical precision in code snippets, mathematical formulas, and architectural trade-off analyses.
- **Authoritativeness:** Anchored by transparent open-source links and Schema.org `Person` sameAs references.
- **Trustworthiness:** Privacy-conscious contact flow, verified contact methods, and zero tracking cookies.
