## 2. Detailed Engagement Specifications

### 1. Technical Systems Engineering & Full-Stack Products
- **Target Audience:** Engineering teams, founders, and technical product owners.
- **Scope:** Complete implementation of data-intensive web apps, multi-tenant databases, typed API controllers, and stateful frontend workflows.
- **Evidence Anchor:** `curasphere-hms`, `multi-agent-prospect-intelligence`.

### 2. Applied Research & Quantitative Modeling
- **Target Audience:** Quantitative researchers, data science teams, and financial technologists.
- **Scope:** Feature engineering on non-stationary financial data, market regime classification, deep learning models (LSTM/GRU), and backtest risk evaluation.
- **Evidence Anchor:** `deep-learning-stock-return-prediction`, `market-regimes`.

### 3. 0-to-1 Technical MVP & Architecture Prototyping
- **Target Audience:** Early-stage founders and technical teams seeking to validate novel algorithmic or data workflows.
- **Scope:** Building lightweight CLI utilities, stateful prototypes, and empirical benchmarks to de-risk core technical assumptions before scaling.
- **Evidence Anchor:** `market-regime-engine`, `fractional-diff-cli`.

### 4. Architecture Review & Technical Exploration
- **Target Audience:** Tech leads and engineers preparing systems for production.
- **Scope:** Pipeline auditing (lookahead leakage verification), Pydantic validation interceptor design, frontend performance optimization (Zero-CLS), and WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility reviews.
- **Evidence Anchor:** `agentic-systems`, `css-subgrid-editorial-alignment`.
