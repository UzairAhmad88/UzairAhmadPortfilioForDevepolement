# Content Requirements & Copywriting Strategy

This document outlines the required, optional, and future content for every section of the portfolio, establishes copywriting standards, and marks explicit `[CONTENT REQUIRED]` placeholders where personal inputs are needed.

---

## 1. Content Writing Rules & Tone of Voice

### A. Tone of Voice
- **Technical & Exact**: Describe architectures, frameworks, and models by their precise technical terminology (e.g. *"PyTorch neural model with multi-horizon evaluation"*, not *"cutting-edge magic AI"*).
- **Evidence-Based**: Focus on what was built, what decisions were made, and what tradeoffs occurred.
- **Human & Confident**: Write in clear, active first-person or neutral technical voice. Avoid corporate boilerplate.
- **Concise**: Deliver high-density information without fluff or narrative padding.

### B. Prohibited Marketing Clichés
Do not use the following terms unless accompanied by specific, verifiable technical facts:
- ❌ *"Passionate developer with a drive for excellence"*
- ❌ *"Revolutionizing industries with next-gen solutions"*
- ❌ *"World-class AI innovator"*
- ❌ *"100% satisfaction guaranteed"*
- ❌ *"Full-stack rockstar / ninja"*
