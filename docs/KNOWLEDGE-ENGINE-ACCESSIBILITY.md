# Knowledge Engine Accessibility Specification

## 1. Multi-Modal Accessibility Compliance
- **Text-First Relationships:** Every pathway and exploration item provides a textual title, descriptive summary, and explicit relationship reason (WCAG 1.1.1 & 1.3.1).
- **Keyboard Traversal:** Pathways and related item links are navigable via standard `Tab` / `Enter` keys with `:focus-visible` offset rings.
- **Screen Reader Announcements:** Contextual links are labeled with clear destination and relational intent (e.g. `"Next step in pathway: Multi-Agent Prospect Intelligence"`).
