# Lab Experiment Taxonomy & Categorization

**Project:** Personal Engineering & Research Platform — Lab Subsystem  
**Reference:** `src/types/lab.ts`  
**Status:** Canonical Taxonomy  

---

## 1. Allowed Experiment Types

- **Algorithm Experiment:** Mathematical or computational transformations (e.g., `fractional-diff-cli`).
- **Quant Experiment:** Quantitative modeling, regime detection, or derivatives calibration (e.g., `gmm-regime-stability-probe`, `stochastic-volatility-heston-calibration`).
- **AI/ML Experiment:** Multi-agent architectures, LLM orchestration, or state machines (e.g., `multi-agent-pydantic-state-machine`).
- **UI Experiment:** Layout engines, CSS subgrids, or rendering performance (e.g., `css-subgrid-editorial-alignment`).
- **Prototype:** Standalone architectural prototypes or event servers (e.g., `streaming-orderbook-sse`).
- **Proof of Concept:** Feasibility studies and early-stage probes.

---

## 2. Allowed Status Lifecycle

- **ACTIVE:** Ongoing exploration currently under active development.
- **PROTOTYPE:** Working prototype exists; evaluation ongoing.
- **VALIDATING:** Testing, benchmarking, and empirical validation underway.
- **COMPLETED:** Exploration reached its defined endpoint.
- **EXPLORING:** Early-stage conceptual exploration.
- **PAUSED / ARCHIVED / ABANDONED:** Intentionally concluded or parked with documented findings.
