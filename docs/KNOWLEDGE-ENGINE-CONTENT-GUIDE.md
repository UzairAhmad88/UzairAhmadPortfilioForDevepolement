# Knowledge Engine Content & Relationship Guide

## 1. Relationship Philosophy
The engine models real, empirical connections formed during system engineering and research investigations. It explicitly distinguishes between:
1. **Fact:** "Project A uses FastAPI" (Verifiable codebase dependency).
2. **Derived Connection:** "Note X documents an architectural lesson from Project A" (Direct editorial attribution).
3. **Curated Pathway:** "Signal Research -> Fractional Diff CLI -> Stock Prediction Platform" (Chronological/methodological evolution).

---

## 2. Inadmissible Patterns
- **No Fictional AI Inferences:** No hallucinated "similarity scores" or probabilistic guesses presented as facts.
- **No Skill Ratings:** Zero skill percentages (`Python: 90%`) or artificial competency levels.
- **No Career Predictions:** No speculative future trajectory or compensation estimations.
- **No User Tracking:** Relationships reflect Uzair's body of work, not visitor behavioral profiling.
