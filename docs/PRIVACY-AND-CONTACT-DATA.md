# Privacy & Contact Data Governance

This document describes the privacy posture, data collection boundaries, and transmission mechanisms of the portfolio website.

## 1. Minimal Data Collection

The website operates on a **zero-tracking, minimal data footprint** philosophy:
- **No Third-Party Tracker Cookies**: The site does not set invasive behavioral tracking cookies or third-party advertising pixels.
- **Inquiry Data Only**: Information submitted via the contact form (Name, Email, Organization, Message) is collected solely to respond to the specific inquiry.

## 2. Data Transmission & Storage

- **No Permanent Web Database Storage**: Contact submissions are transmitted directly via encrypted HTTPS to the configured transactional email provider and delivered to Uzair Ahmad's secure inbox. Submissions are not stored in an open web database.
- **Retention**: Inquiries are retained in email records strictly for ongoing professional correspondence and deleted upon request.
- **Third-Party Processing**: If a transactional email gateway (e.g. Resend, Formspree) is configured, it acts as a data processor strictly for message delivery.

## 3. Contact Data Subject Rights

Senders may request deletion or inspection of their submitted correspondence at any time by emailing `imuzairahmad8@gmail.com`.
