## 2. Area Breakdown

### Area 1: Quantitative Systems & Financial Engineering
- **Domain:** Quantitative Finance & Statistical ML
- **Core Problems:** Non-stationary signal stationarization, temporal memory loss, lookahead bias in cross-validation, regime-dependent volatility clustering.
- **Evidence Linked:**
  - Projects: `/work/deep-learning-stock-return-prediction`, `/work/market-regime-engine`
  - Research: `/research/signal-research`, `/research/market-regimes`
  - Lab: `/lab/fractional-diff-cli`, `/lab/gmm-regime-stability-probe`, `/lab/streaming-orderbook-sse`
- **Canonical Tech:** Python, PyTorch, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn

### Area 2: Multi-Agent AI & Deterministic Workflows
- **Domain:** Artificial Intelligence & Systems Architecture
- **Core Problems:** Stochastic failure modes in autonomous agents, JSON schema non-compliance, infinite critique-revision loops, rate-limited I/O orchestration.
- **Evidence Linked:**
  - Projects: `/work/multi-agent-prospect-intelligence`
  - Research: `/research/agentic-systems`
  - Lab: `/lab/multi-agent-pydantic-state-machine`
- **Canonical Tech:** LangGraph, FastAPI, Pydantic, TypeScript, Python

### Area 3: Distributed Web & Operations Platforms
- **Domain:** Full-Stack Software Engineering
- **Core Problems:** Relational data integrity across multi-tenant systems, asynchronous database session lifecycles, accessible DOM design, layout shifts during data rendering.
- **Evidence Linked:**
  - Projects: `/work/curasphere-hms`
  - Lab: `/lab/css-subgrid-editorial-alignment`
- **Canonical Tech:** React, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Astro, TailwindCSS, HTML5/CSS3

### Area 4: Technical Prototyping & Lab Benchmarks
- **Domain:** Applied Research & 0-to-1 Systems
- **Core Problems:** De-risking algorithmic assumptions before full architecture builds, evaluating streaming network overhead (SSE vs WebSocket), mathematical calibration convergence under illiquid regimes.
- **Evidence Linked:**
  - Projects: `/work/market-regime-engine`, `/work/deep-learning-stock-return-prediction`
  - Research: `/research/signal-research`
  - Lab: `/lab/fractional-diff-cli`, `/lab/streaming-orderbook-sse`, `/lab/stochastic-volatility-heston-calibration`
- **Canonical Tech:** Python, NumPy, Scikit-Learn, Astro

## 3. Unsupported / Excluded Areas
To maintain truthfulness and avoid generic agency traps, the portfolio explicitly excludes:
- Generic content marketing sites & WordPress theme tweaking.
- Unconstrained AI hype projects lacking ground-truth metrics.
- Unverified proprietary black-box software without source code.
