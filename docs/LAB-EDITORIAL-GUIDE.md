# Lab Editorial & Technical Voice Guide

**Project:** Personal Engineering & Research Platform — Lab Subsystem  
**Scope:** Voice, Tone, Style, and Technical Precision  
**Status:** Canonical Standard  

---

## 1. Tone of Voice

- **First-Person Technical Notebook:** Authentic, reflective, and precise.
- **Honest & Direct:** Directly discuss boundary conditions, failure modes, and performance trade-offs.
- **Prohibited Language:** Avoid breathless marketing adjectives ("revolutionary", "lightning-fast", "industry-leading", "seamless").
- **Preferred Verbs:** "Evaluated", "Observed", "Benchmarked", "Enforced", "Truncated", "Calibrated", "Permuted".

---

## 2. Terminology & Acronym Consistency

- Always use canonical terminology:
  - `Server-Sent Events` or `SSE` (not "server-events" or "SSE streamings")
  - `WebSockets` (plural for protocol, WebSocket for API)
  - `HTTP/2` (not "Http2" or "HTTP 2.0")
  - `NumPy`, `FastAPI`, `LangGraph`, `Pydantic` (proper capitalization)
