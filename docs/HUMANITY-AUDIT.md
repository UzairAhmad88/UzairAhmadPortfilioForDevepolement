## 2. Narrative Tone & First-Person Voice

- **Writing Perspective:** Direct, confident first-person engineering voice ("I built", "I investigated", "I discovered").
- **Engineering Retrospectives:** Explicit sections for **Challenges**, **Empirical Limitations**, and **Lessons Learned** across all case studies prove authentic hands-on implementation experience.
- **Uncertainty & Open Inquiry:** Research dossiers separate factual backtest findings from analytical interpretation and explicitly list open theoretical questions.
