# Identity Governance & Brand Integrity Policy

**Project:** Personal Developer Portfolio / Personal Engineering & Research Platform  
**Stage:** Post-Production Governance & Long-Term Stewardship  
**Target Identity:** Uzair Ahmad — Quantitative AI & Product Engineer  

---

## 1. Core Identity Pillars

The platform's identity is defined by 5 enduring qualities:

1. **Technical Credibility:** Grounded in mathematical rigor, deep learning architectures, time-series forecasting, and production software engineering.
2. **First-Person Authenticity:** Direct, humble, and precise engineering voice ("I built", "I investigated", "I discovered").
3. **Editorial & Visual Restraint:** Clean, high-contrast HSL color palettes, crisp typography (Inter & JetBrains Mono), and purposeful whitespace.
4. **Evidence-First Architecture:** Open-source GitHub code repositories, live deployment links, and verifiable test suites over marketing claims.
5. **Continuous Inquiry:** Demonstrating not merely what was built, but *how the engineer thinks, frames questions, and makes architectural decisions*.

## 3. The Identity Test for Future Content

Before publishing any new page or copy update, perform the **Blind Identity Test**:

> *"If my name and URL were stripped from this text, would an engineering leader immediately recognize that this belongs to a serious, thoughtful engineer with deep expertise in quantitative systems and software architecture?"*

If the answer is **NO**, refine the narrative until it reflects authentic, specific technical truth.
