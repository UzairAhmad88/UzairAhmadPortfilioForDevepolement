## Knowledge Classification Principles

1. **Research**: Structured inquiries containing formal hypotheses, empirical validation methodologies, datasets, mathematical formulations, and explicit limitations.
2. **Project Documentation**: In-depth implementation guides, system architectural blueprints, and engineering trade-off analyses.
3. **Technical Note**: Focused explanations of singular engineering problems, configuration architectures, or deployment mechanics.
4. **Concept Explanation**: Rigorous breakdown of mathematical, statistical, or machine learning theorems and formulas.
5. **Study Note**: Coursework exploration or curated technical learning notes properly distinguished from novel primary research.
